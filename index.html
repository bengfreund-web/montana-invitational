FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Who can enter?

Any U19 or U16 high school or club program, boys or girls. Teams travel in from across the country. In 2026 sides came from Montana, Utah, Idaho, Washington, Oregon, California, Texas and Hawaii.

What is the format?

XVs across two days at Bozeman Sports Park. Day 1 is two pool matches, Day 2 is semifinals and finals. Every team is guaranteed four matches.

How long are the matches?

45 minutes, played as 22.5 minute halves.

What are the divisions?

Six: HS Boys Single School, HS Boys Club Elite, HS Boys Club Premier, HS Girls Club Elite, HS Girls Club Premier, and U16 Boys Elite.

How is seeding decided?

Off GRR rankings where teams are ranked. Unranked teams are seeded by geography so local sides are not drawn against each other in pool play.

What does the $995 cover?

Entry for one team across both days, including four guaranteed matches. Travel, lodging and meals are not included.

How many teams are in the field?

Fifty across all five divisions.

Where is it played?

Bozeman Sports Park in Bozeman, Montana. Four full sized grass pitches plus a purpose built turf rugby field.

How do we get there?

Fly into Bozeman Yellowstone International (BZN), about 8 minutes from the venue. Butte, Helena and Billings are the next closest airports.

Where do we stay?

Through HBC Event Services, the tournament's lodging partner. Rates and availability are on the Find Your Hotel link. If booking is unavailable, contact HBC directly at 505-346-0522.

Can we referee?

Yes. The tournament covers referee kit, twin share lodging and meals. Apply through the referee page.

Is there merchandise?
